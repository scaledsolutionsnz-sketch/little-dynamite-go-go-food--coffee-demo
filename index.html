Our little shop

A proper local pie shop, run with heart

Little Dynamite Go Go Food & Coffee sits right on Main Road in Riwaka. Under friendly new ownership, Leanne makes all the pies, savouries and sweets herself, so what's in the cabinet each day is genuinely handmade, not trucked in.

Locals, cyclists and travellers heading to Kaiteriteri and the Abel Tasman all pull in for the same thing: an award-winning pie, a good coffee and a warm welcome. Come as you are, we'll sort you out.

  • Owner-made, baked on-sitePies, savouries and sweets made fresh by hand every day.
  • Award-winning piesThe pies people cross town for, and stop mid-road-trip for.
  • Quick, takeaway-friendlyOrder at the counter, grab your coffee and go, no fuss.
