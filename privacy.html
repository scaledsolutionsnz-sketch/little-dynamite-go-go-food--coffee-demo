Privacy Policy

How we handle your information when you contact us or use this website.

1. Who we are

Little Dynamite Go Go Food & Coffee is a café on Main Road, Riwaka, Nelson Tasman, New Zealand. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

We only collect personal information you choose to give us, for example, your name, phone number or email address when you call or email us to place an order or ask a question. This website does not use contact forms.

  • Contact details you provide when you phone or email us.
  • Basic, anonymous website analytics (such as pages visited), if enabled.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, fulfil an order, and provide the food and service you've asked for. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where necessary to operate our business (for example, our phone or email provider) or where required by law.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us from loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Contact us and we'll help.
